Finance · Time-Series

Stock Market Price Predictor

Forecasts stock closing prices using about 10 years of Google (GOOG) data from Yahoo Finance. A 4-layer LSTM model in Keras is trained on 100-day windows with an 80/20 train–test split. A Streamlit app plots predicted vs. actual prices alongside 50/100/200-day moving averages, which taught me how LSTM forecasting works and where it falls short.
